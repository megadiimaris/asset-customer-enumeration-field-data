# Asset & Customer Enumeration Field Data Management – Revenue Generation Systems

## 📋 Project Overview

This project was executed during my role as **Field Manager** at **Revenue Generation Systems** for the Asset and Customer Enumeration Project at **Jos Electricity Distribution Plc**.

The objective was to conduct comprehensive **field data collection**, **verification**, and **mapping** of electricity distribution assets and customers across the utility's network. This involved using **Geographic Information System (GIS)** tools for asset tagging, customer location verification, and data quality assurance to support business planning and operational decision-making.

### Field Operations & Data Collection

- Supervised the identification, tagging, and mapping of distribution assets and customer locations
